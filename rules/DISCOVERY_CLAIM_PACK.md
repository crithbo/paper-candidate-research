# Discovery Claim-Pack Protocol

Protocol revision: `DISCOVERY-CLAIM-PACK-V9-R0`

Activation is assignment-pinned: `PRODUCTION` is canonical only with a registered cutover; `SHADOW` is noncanonical and `OFF` preserves the frozen legacy method. Current mode/cutover status belongs in registry/assignment, not this protocol. Backtest or shadow output is process evidence, never candidate novelty, headroom or feasibility evidence.

## Unit of work and immutable gates

The unit of work is an `OPPORTUNITY_FAMILY`; the deliverable is a falsifiable `RESEARCH_CLAIM_PACKAGE`. Proposal count is capacity telemetry, never a quota. All Q2 minimum, Q1 priority, same-object, latest/current collision, fair current strong baseline, natural/canonical evidence, full-cost, reproducibility, evidence honesty, claim ceiling, STOP non-revival and user Stage B/formal-project gates remain unchanged.

A claim package freezes:

1. exact research object and same-function/protocol/quality contract;
2. natural workload/scenario or canonical real formal family;
3. observed problem or opportunity and its source-backed quantitative/static anchor;
4. intervention and atomic action;
5. claimed mechanism and mechanism-specific prediction;
6. available/unavailable information and assumptions;
7. named current strong comparator set;
8. expected output, effect target or guarantee;
9. complete decision-relevant full-cost boundary;
10. scope, no-gain/failure region and strongest competing explanation;
11. minimum falsification test and finite Stage A closure route;
12. contribution type, N1/N2/N3 route, dependencies, resources and evidence ceiling.

## Contribution-type contracts

Evaluate the relevant type contract before mapping to the project's internal Q1/Q2 tier:

- `METHOD_ALGORITHM`: problem → nontrivial action/mechanism → fair comparator → measurable effect/guarantee → boundary.
- `SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE`: real constraint → mechanism → end-to-end route → performance/power/area/bandwidth/cost → fair baseline.
- `COMPILER_TOOL`: new capability/automation scope → correctness → integration → compile/runtime/data-movement cost → real workload.
- `MEASUREMENT_CHARACTERIZATION`: new phenomenon → reproducible measurement → generality/conditional distribution → consequence → explanation or design implication.
- `BENCHMARK_DATASET`: construct → coverage → data quality → discriminative ability → real research need.
- `THEORY_FORMAL`: object/assumptions → result/guarantee → proof route → correspondence to practice.
- `REPLICATION_NEGATIVE`: prior boundary → rigorous replication → inconsistency/failure condition → substantive implication.

Q1/Q2 is an internal paper-shape and investment mapping, not a universal cross-field score. A type contract that is incomplete cannot be repaired by venue prestige, prose quality, AI readiness or a numeric score.

## Opportunity-family graph

Every family has a stable `opportunity_family_id`, a canonical signature and typed edges. The signature covers object, problem, action, mechanism, information, output/guarantee, comparator and full-cost boundary. Legal edge types are:

- `EQUIVALENT`
- `CONTAINS`
- `CONDITIONAL_SPECIALIZATION`
- `ALTERNATE_MECHANISM`
- `COMPLEMENTARY`
- `PREREQUISITE`
- `COMPETING_EXPLANATION`
- `INDEPENDENT`

Flat keyword or title similarity never proves equivalence. A family may hold multiple claim packages until one is selected, merged, narrowed or structurally removed. Cross-lane dedup locks the family ID and signature, not a paper title.

## Local audit coordinates

These coordinates are Discovery-local metadata and do not replace registry lifecycle state:

- Academic maturity: `A0_STRUCTURAL_NO / A1_OPEN_WITHIN_EVIDENCE / A2_RESEARCH_SPACE_ARTICULABLE / A3_FALSIFIABLE_CONTRIBUTION_CLAIM`.
- Collision: `C0_DIRECT_COVERED / C1_PARTIALLY_COVERED_NARROWED / C2_ADJACENT_DISTINCT / C3_NO_DIRECT_COVER_WITHIN_RECORDED_SEARCH / CU_UNKNOWN`.
- Evidence: `D0_CLUE / D1_BASIC_MATERIALS / D2_DECISION_READY_FACETS / D3_INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE_AUDIT`.
- Execution readiness: `E0_UNKNOWN / E1_STATIC_PREFLIGHT / E2_ENGINEERING_SMOKE / EB_RESOURCE_OR_ENGINEERING_BLOCKED / EH_HUMAN_CORE`.

The axes cannot override one another. E1/E2 never upgrades A/C/D; low execution readiness never makes an academically viable topic scientific DROP/STOP.

## Direct coverage and structural negatives

`C0_DIRECT_COVERED` requires evidence that one current work or a fair deployable union satisfies all eight predicates:

1. same object/function/protocol;
2. same scenario/workload class;
3. same or weaker information assumptions;
4. action or decision rule covers the proposed atomic action;
5. output legality, quality and guarantee are the same or stronger;
6. full-cost boundary is the same and not conclusion-changing worse;
7. scale, platform, version and configuration are comparable;
8. coverage is supported by sufficient full-text, current implementation or reproducible observation, not a snippet or naming overlap.

If any predicate is unresolved, use `C1/C2/CU`, narrow the residual and keep an explicit claim ceiling. Action-space overlap alone is not algorithm absorption.

A structural DROP is legal only when evidence establishes at least one of:

- object/problem/guarantee is internally inconsistent or violates same-object;
- the central claim is not falsifiable;
- the apparent gain exists only by deleting decisive cost or weakening the guarantee;
- all eight direct-coverage predicates hold;
- the delta is only naming, tuning, wrapper, application/backend relabeling or a nongeneral special case;
- no testable mechanism chain can be stated;
- the claim requires information unavailable under its own contract;
- finite fidelity closure necessarily changes object, function, guarantee or fair comparator.

The following are not structural negatives: missing implementation/result/proof; missing compute/license/data/platform; AI capability limits; incomplete search; adjacent or partial coverage; a human-core proof or experiment; resource transport failure. Use `NOT_ADMITTED_UNFROZEN`, `SEARCH_BOUNDED_OPEN`, `EB`, `EH` or an actionable blocker as appropriate.

## Source roles, search ledger and claim audit

Decision-ready work must cover these source roles, with `NOT_FOUND_WITHIN_SEARCH` permitted when the bounded search is recorded:

1. paper genealogy and forward/backward chaining;
2. current first-party documentation, source, defaults and non-default controls;
3. natural phenomena, measurements, workloads or canonical formal carrier;
4. generic algorithm/formal kernel;
5. independent prior-work and contrary/no-gain evidence;
6. synthesis, assumption attack and execution preflight.

For the highest-priority family, the decisive prior-work/current-source search is performed by a lane or reviewer not responsible for generating the package. A shared summary or copied query is not independent review.

The evidence ledger records each query's text, purpose, source/search engine, date, filters, read depth, key results, exclusions and state change. Normalize preprint/conference/journal versions; repository/branch/tag/commit; default/non-default configurations; dataset version/license/checksum/date; and online-document access date or snapshot.

Each material claim records source, locator, version, role (`DIRECT / PARTIAL / SUPPORT / OPPOSE / IMPLEMENTS / BASELINE / CARRIER / BACKGROUND`), read depth, evidence status (`AUTHOR_CLAIM / DIRECT_OBSERVATION / INDEPENDENT_REPLICATION / SYSTEM_INFERENCE / UNVERIFIED`), relation, conflict and permitted conclusion. Search snippets locate sources but cannot support decisive claims.

Check priority is ordinal, not pseudo-probabilistic: expected chance of changing the decision × consequence ÷ check cost, reported as `HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW` with rationale.

## Decision-ready stopping and reopening

A family reaches `D2_DECISION_READY_FACETS` only when:

- every high-impact claim/uncertainty is answered or explicitly bounded;
- latest same-object work and current strong comparator/source/configuration union are checked;
- first-party implementation is read to sufficient depth where action absence matters;
- at least one heterogeneous independent search path is completed;
- contrary/no-gain/competing-explanation search is recorded;
- natural/canonical carrier and full-cost boundary are frozen;
- repeated new sources add no decision-changing facet within the registered search boundary;
- no unresolved contradiction could immediately flip A/C or the structural disposition.

Budget exhaustion does not force a yes/no decision. It yields `SEARCH_BOUNDED_OPEN`, `NOT_ADMITTED_UNFROZEN`, `RESOURCE_BLOCKED`, `EXPERT_REVIEW_NEEDED` or `DEEP_DIVE_REQUIRED`. Mainline samples low-ranked families for false-negative audit.

Reopen triggers for a nonterminal opportunity family include a new same-object paper, new official feature/configuration, new natural dataset/workload, a Stage 0 repair, a later observation that falsifies an assumption, or a material platform/cost change. A terminal STOP candidate never reopens or changes name; a genuinely different object/problem receives a new ID.

## E1 and E2

`E1_STATIC_PREFLIGHT` is read-only: artifact availability, version/license, build/run entry points, dependencies, interfaces, format/checker semantics, rough resource bound and known blocker. It does not require environment mutation.

`E2_ENGINEERING_SMOKE` is optional and only legal with an assignment-level allowlist for commands, bytes, time, installation, network, write directory and cleanup. Allowed outputs are environment/container build status, official unit-test/minimal-example status, one input read/check, baseline startup, output/checker parity, tiny semantic correctness and order-of-magnitude resource feasibility. It must not run a parameter sweep, compare candidate scientific performance, optimize a mechanism or support a scientific claim. Failure updates execution readiness/blocker only; absence of E2 never blocks Stage 0.

## Neutral review and activation

Mainline renders claim-pack fields in a fixed order for Stage 0. Remove model self-confidence, persuasive adjectives, source-lane identity and venue prestige as evidence. Do not hide technical facts or provenance.

Before `PRODUCTION`, independent PACKER→EXECUTOR→AUDITOR evaluation must include paired same-snapshot legacy/V9 runs, contamination controls, at least three contribution types, positive/structural-negative/partial-coverage/unfinished cases, a new blind holdout, style/evidence-order/adversarial perturbations and two prospective noncanonical shadow waves. Pre-register noninferiority margins, sample expansion/stopping and adjudication. A six-case replay or two waves alone is a pilot, not calibration.

Method pass requires no hard-gate leakage, no terminal-revival behavior, no E2-to-academic leakage, noninferior critical-source recall and false-structural-DROP under the preregistered uncertainty rule, and acceptable audit cost. Failure returns mode `OFF`; no candidate state rolls back. A 3-vs-6 lane-count comparison is a separate scheduling experiment.
