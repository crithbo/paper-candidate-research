# Archived Discovery runbook and dormant R7 notes

The live `ROLE_DISCOVERY.md` previously duplicated the registered `research-topic-discovery` skill: eight divergence engines, perspective rotation, seed clustering, RQ convergence, pre-RQ nomination, three-pass genealogy/mechanism/collision steps, and a dormant R7 collision-first mode. These operational steps were removed from the role rule on 2026-08-30. The skill and its references remain the canonical runbook; the role remains the authority for permissions, evidence-qualified raw fields, scientific gates, outputs and handoff.

R7 used `DISCOVERY_QUALITY_MODE=OFF / SHADOW / PRODUCTION`, atomic action signatures, bounded collision-first queries, failure-kernel lookup and prebrief adversarial outcomes. It was never the current canonical method and may not be restored as a parallel mode. Any future use must explicitly replace or merge with the current funnel and retire superseded definitions.

The old three-pass mnemonic asked for problem genealogy, competing mechanisms and an execution/collision entry. Those obligations remain covered by the skill's anchor/RQ/source-role/deep-review workflow and the live raw/brief contracts.
