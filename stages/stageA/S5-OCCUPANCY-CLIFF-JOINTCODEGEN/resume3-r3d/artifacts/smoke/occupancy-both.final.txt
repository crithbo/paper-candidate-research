llvm-calc-occupancy - AMDGPU occupancy calculator

Target
  Triple:              amdgpu-amd-amdhsa
  GPU (-mcpu):         gfx90a
  Wavefront size:      64
  Max waves/EU:        8 (waves per SIMD, hardware limit)
  EUs (SIMDs) per CU:  4
  LDS per CU:          65536 bytes (64 KiB)
  Addressable LDS:     65536 bytes (64 KiB) per workgroup

Inputs
  Workgroup size:      1 .. 1024 (unspecified -> full range)
  VGPRs per lane:      80
  SGPRs per wave:      102
  LDS per workgroup:   unspecified (0)

Per-constraint occupancy (waves/EU)
  Workgroup + LDS:     8
  VGPRs:               6
  SGPRs:               6

Result
  Occupancy:           6 waves/EU (24 waves/CU)
  Limited by:          VGPRs, SGPRs
  Next step:           reach 7 waves/EU (requires all of):
      VGPRs <= 72 (currently 80)
      SGPRs <= 96 (currently 102)
