llvm-calc-occupancy - AMDGPU occupancy calculator

Target
  Triple:              amdgpu-amd-amdhsa
  GPU (-mcpu):         gfx1030
  Wavefront size:      32
  Max waves/EU:        16 (waves per SIMD, hardware limit)
  EUs (SIMDs) per CU:  4
  LDS per CU:          131072 bytes (128 KiB)
  Addressable LDS:     65536 bytes (64 KiB) per workgroup

Inputs
  Workgroup size:      1 .. 1024 (unspecified -> full range)
  VGPRs per lane:      128
  SGPRs per wave:      unspecified
  LDS per workgroup:   unspecified (0)

Per-constraint occupancy (waves/EU)
  Workgroup + LDS:     16
  VGPRs:               8

Result
  Occupancy:           8 waves/EU (32 waves/CU)
  Limited by:          VGPRs
  Next step:           reach 9 waves/EU:
      VGPRs <= 112 (currently 128)
