## Executive judgment

The owner correctly failed closed before any scientific probe. The frozen public corpus establishes a real mobile foreground/LLM coexistence problem and the existence of transaction-level QoS fields, but it does not instantiate the exact same-object experiment required by the contract: a natural, per-request source-to-first-shared-arbiter mapping; the actual native mobile comparator's rules and parameters; common source/budget/quality denominators; unit-bearing full-cost paths; and a small legality/equivalence witness covering both candidate and B4.

This is not a PASS because there is no claim-bearing evidence. It is not a STOP because there is no same-object absorption, natural counterexample, formal refutation, full-cost zero residual, or direct fatal collision. It is not `REVISE_ONCE` because an atomic change to the frozen mechanism, model, comparator or evidence plan cannot manufacture the missing natural cross-layer mapping without either obtaining new human research evidence or drifting to another object. It is not `BLOCKED_USER_ACTION_REQUIRED` because the public-source acquisition completed and no finite known file, permission, license, device, endpoint or tool retry is the missing step. The missing work is the durable human research core itself: platform access, cross-layer causal annotation, interpretation or attestation of native comparator semantics, and physical cost calibration.

Therefore the unique evidence-consistent disposition is `CONFIRM_RESERVE_RECOMMENDED`.

## Claim and novelty-route consistency

The frozen conditional proposition remains narrow and coherent: on the same natural mobile UI/LLM contention object, a source- and budget-preserving request-level contract at the first shared NoC/DRAM arbiter must yield at least one equal-quality, full-cost, starvation-bounded non-dominated point relative to a same-information native transaction-QoS baseline.

The possible N1 residual is not “a new QoS tag.” Arm's transaction QoS fields are a direct subtractor. The residual survives only if provenance, deadline/budget semantics and a legal arbiter action are preserved in a way that the actual same-information native comparator cannot express or absorb. That question remains untested. The current claim ceiling is therefore only a conditional mechanism shape and a falsifiable research design; no performance, deployability, vendor-interface, causal, theorem or novelty-completion claim is supported.

## Decisive evidence audit

The preclaim gate's failure is scientifically material and well targeted:

1. Sereno supplies the natural commercial-phone coexistence setting and topology-level attribution, but not a complete immutable per-request route/action stream from source operation to the named first shared arbiter.
2. Arm AXI specifies 4-bit `AWQOS`/`ARQOS` identifiers per transaction and explicitly does not define their exact system use; this cannot instantiate the native comparator.
3. The public corpus does not jointly expose object identity, request provenance, native QoS values/decisions, candidate/B4 action coverage, equal-quality denominators and full-cost paths on one natural witness.
4. XSched acts at a command-queue boundary and Layerweaver+ at request/layer scheduling boundaries; neither supplies the frozen first-shared-arbiter action.

The failure occurred before candidate, baseline, proxy, synthetic, Pareto or performance execution. Accordingly it is an evidence-readiness finding, not a result about the mechanism.
