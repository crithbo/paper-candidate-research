## Outcome first

The owner STOP recommendation is not confirmed. The package contains real and valuable negative evidence, but the evidence answers a narrower proxy question than the frozen Stage 0 proposition. The natural harness assigns access weights through a hard-coded schema-field function (`32, 24, 16, ...`) rather than a frozen training-only request trace mapped to logical endpoint identifiers. Those defects are coupled parts of one measurement-fidelity failure. They do not negate the candidate mechanism, and they do not prove that the trace-profiled frozen mechanism lacks an access or byte residual. Stage A still has its one scientific revision available, so the correct disposition is one finite, atomic `REVISE_ONCE`, not STOP, BLOCKED, reserve, or PASS.

## Baseline fairness audit

The eight arms are individually executable and appear exactly once per model: generated Pack, stable topo, dedup-off, size-matched capacity, hot-first, local field order, generic matched-width beam, and FB-RCFDP. All 192 rows pass verifier/equality/identifier/alignment, and all eight rows within each model share the same canonical digest. This is strong same-object evidence.

The scalar residual recomputation is arithmetically correct: for each metric, the candidate is compared with the minimum eligible comparator value for that model. Such a coordinate-wise union is legitimate for statements such as “no byte win against any arm.” It is not one executable comparator point and therefore cannot by itself establish Pareto dominance or absence of a full-cost nondominated candidate point.

The owner analyzer never performs that frontier test. `scripts/analyze_results.py:69` computes independent per-metric minima, while line 169 assigns `qualifying_full_cost_nondominated_at_required_heldout_p50_p90` to literal `False`. An independent five-measured-dimension check found a single comparator dominating the candidate on only `6/24` models, not `24/24`. This does not rescue the candidate, but it proves the reported full-cost frontier verdict was not derived from the recorded vectors.
