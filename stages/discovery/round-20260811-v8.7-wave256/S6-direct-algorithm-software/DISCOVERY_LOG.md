# Discovery log

Six fresh current-heuristic construction families were screened before deep
review. Witness-first eliminated Graphviz and igraph because the purported
fixed object changes with the layout/community quality objective, and OpenFST
because routine representation/order choices are already within the native
exact-algorithm baseline.

Three remaining families received candidate-grade reviews: METIS multilevel
partitioning, PostgreSQL hash-index split/build, and Brotli meta-block/context
construction. Each has a legal action-divergence witness and a finite natural
route, but none has a closed complete current union plus primary direct-collision
matrix. The honest outcome is `COMPLETE_ZERO_PROPOSALS` with three
`NOT_ADMITTED_UNFROZEN` records.

No experiments, benchmarks, downloads or Stage work were performed. No claim is
made about empirical gains or the absence of current functionality.
