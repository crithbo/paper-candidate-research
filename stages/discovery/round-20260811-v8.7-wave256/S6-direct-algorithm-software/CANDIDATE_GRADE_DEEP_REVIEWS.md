# Candidate-grade deep reviews

## METIS

Official upstream is the METIS source tree; its multilevel algorithm is a strong
native baseline, not a blank heuristic. The same-object witness establishes
multiple legal coarsening/refinement histories only. It does not establish that
the current configuration/action union excludes a proposed target-specific
algorithm, nor that direct partitioning literature has not already absorbed the
guarantee. **Status: `NOT_ADMITTED_UNFROZEN`.** Finite killer: pin source and
all options, replay its full composition on one public weighted graph, then
reject if the candidate is expressible, uses generic matching/ILP, or has no
charged cut/balance construction advantage.

## PostgreSQL hash index

Official PostgreSQL source and documentation expose native hash access methods,
but this review did not freeze all build, split, WAL, vacuum and configuration
paths. A split-history witness is a legitimate same-query action divergence,
not a proof of an unexpressed global residual. Extensible-hashing and database
index construction are direct collision families. **Status:
`NOT_ADMITTED_UNFROZEN`.** Finite killer: source-pin paths/options, build two
native legal indexes, validate `amcheck` and query equality, replay current
union, and reject on absorption or full-cost dominance.

## Brotli

The official Brotli encoder/decoder supports a fixed decoded-byte contract and
has nontrivial meta-block/context modelling; that alone is insufficient for a
paper-grade residual. The review cannot yet enumerate all encoder modes,
heuristics and existing constrained-compression algorithms. **Status:
`NOT_ADMITTED_UNFROZEN`.** Finite killer: source-pin encode options, use two
decoder-identical encodings, replay current modes with the same byte stream and
budget, then reject if the plan reduces to tuning or lacks a target-specific
guarantee after full encode/decode cost.

No review is dropped because of unimplemented code, unmeasured performance, or
resource availability.
