- This is a narrow re-screen of only the two nonterminal, unproposed Wave37
  directions: Apache Kafka tiered storage and Apache Flink checkpointing.
  SQLite, io_uring, and ClickHouse are deliberately out of scope.

  v8.6 fidelity-plan and v8.7 current-upstream-reality disciplines.  The

## Why neither is a proposal

Neither decision equates a native policy surface with algorithmic absorption.
For Kafka, remote-log, fetch, cache, and retention facilities would be the
strong baseline union; for Flink, aligned, unaligned, timeout/hybrid and
recovery configurations would be the strong baseline union.  The question is
whether their *complete* legal actions, information, guarantee, and full cost
express the proposed rule.  The two ideas do not yet satisfy the preceding
condition that the candidate action itself be fully specified.

Kafka's apparent `hydrate + evict` pair risks being either a lifecycle/cache
wrapper or a change to availability/retention semantics.  Flink's apparent
partial cut risks being either an invalid barrier construction or a different
checkpoint/recovery guarantee.  Those are structural contract failures; they
are not failures to implement, measure, acquire hardware, or obtain an AI
result.

## Current-source reality and source transport

The designated primary current-source routes are Apache Kafka's tiered-storage
documentation and source tree, and Apache Flink's current checkpointing under
backpressure documentation and source tree (listed in the source matrix).
The previously observed official-web transport failure is retained solely as
a readiness limitation.  No implementation-absence, default/flag, or direct
collision assertion in this package is inferred from an unavailable page.
Consequently this package does not claim that either upstream lacks a feature;
it records that the proposed action has not reached a truthful, finite
same-object fidelity contract.

## Required conditions for any future new-ID discovery

- **Kafka:** freeze a versioned upstream comparator; name a legal lease,
  hydration, and release transition that preserves the same consumer fetch
  response, partition offsets, isolation, and retention; and supply a public
  fetch/retention trace plus a finite CPU broker/source-level witness.  The
  killer is: if the strongest-union action can reproduce the same transitions
  and full-cost outcome, or if the rule changes availability/retention, STOP.
- **Flink:** freeze a versioned upstream comparator; prove on a minimal
  backpressured graph that the selective channel-state cut recovers to exactly
  the same checkpointed state; use an official/public job artifact or trace;
  and compare aligned, unaligned, and configured hybrid modes with checkpoint
  duration, channel-state bytes, recovery time, and throughput all charged.
  The killer is: if the cut is illegal, changes the guarantee, or is expressed
  by that full union, STOP.

## Decision

`COMPLETE_ZERO_PROPOSALS`.

The rescreen separates **structural potential** from **current evidence
readiness** and does not convert missing implementation or transport into a
scientific negative.  Yet potential cannot be promoted without a named legal
whole action and a finite fidelity closure.  No earlier terminal object is
revived and no Stage 0 authorization is created.
