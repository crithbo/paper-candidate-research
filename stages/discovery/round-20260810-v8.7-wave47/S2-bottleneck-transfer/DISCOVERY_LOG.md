## Structural-potential versus readiness

Some rows would have strong CPU/natural-artifact readiness in isolation: zstd
has a stable format/reference implementation and common corpora; LLVM and
PostgreSQL have reproducible source tools and public program/query workloads.
That is **not** a substitute for structural potential.  All candidate actions
were already present in the strongest union, were terminal-lineage duplicates,
or reduced to a configuration policy.  Therefore their structural potential is
`BELOW_Q2_STOP` for this precise residual, independent of implementation,
results, resource availability, or AI readiness.
