# Unique opportunity families

Six cross-domain, unregistered families were screened: Lucene BKD construction; PostgreSQL BRIN summarization; LLVM machine block placement; CPython bytecode cache marshalling; OpenJDK class-data sharing layout; and PostgreSQL GiST spatial splitting. They are distinct from the recently screened SQLite/libpng/Tady and S6 index/FST/page-assignment objects.

No family is retained. Three lack a frozen natural repeatability witness and complete action union; three are structurally absorbed or outside the fixed-object direct-constructor scope.
