# Unique opportunity families

Six writer families were screened: MCAP, Matroska, Avro OCF, pcapng, fragmented MP4/CMAF, and ROS 2 bag storage. ROS 2 bag is explicitly deduplicated against MCAP because its storage representation is MCAP; it is not treated as a new opportunity.

No independent family is retained. MCAP's grammar absorbs legal indexed construction, Avro lacks the required multi-state structure, and three candidates lack a frozen current writer union plus natural carrier/witness rather than being rejected for readiness.
