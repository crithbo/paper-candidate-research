## D1/D2 cards

### S16-01 — VTune XPU-offload attribution

- **Estimand:** an application-level, device-attributable bottleneck/ranking statement under fixed application input, software version and hardware topology.
- **Current union / reality check:** current VTune GPU Offload results expose GPU usage/idle, CPU and GPU tasks, occupancy and adapter selection; the current command-line guide defines `xpu-offload` as execution examination on CPU/GPU/NPU cores.  Those views are a strong current comparator, not proof of an unbiased application-level estimate.
- **Confounds and full cost:** device topology/tile identity, clocks and thermal state, host scheduling, collection mode, trace/result size, collection and analysis CPU/RSS/storage, and run duration must all enter the denominator.
- **Required conclusion change:** a pre-registered natural application stratum must cause the stock counter/summary ranking to differ from an independently defined attributable-work ranking; otherwise this is only a tool report.
- **Held-out falsifier:** reserve an application/device stratum before measurement; reject the paper shape if the conclusion does not persist on that stratum after the confounds above are controlled.
- **Why not admitted:** no such versioned natural panel or independent attribution oracle was frozen from first-party sources.  This is an incomplete evidence contract, not a resource, implementation or readiness inference.  The same missing facet may receive at most one future source-only closure under the assignment policy.

### S16-02 — PyTorch trace-window ranking reversal

- **Estimand:** whether a documented profiler schedule changes an operator/kernel bottleneck or model ranking relative to a lower-intrusion reference under identical model/input semantics.
- **Current union / reality check:** `torch.profiler.profile` exposes activities, schedule, output handler, shapes, memory, stacks and device-specific metrics.  `schedule` exposes wait/warmup/active/repeat; current documentation warns that shape/stack tracing adds overhead and can retain tensor references/copies.
- **Confounds and full cost:** warm-up/JIT/compilation, data loading, allocator state, device clocks/thermal state, trace write/analysis CPU/RSS/bytes and intrusive profiler overhead are all required costs.
- **Required conclusion change and held-out falsifier:** pin a public natural model/input panel, declare a conclusion/ranking threshold, and hold out model families or hardware.  Reject if the claimed reversal disappears against the declared lower-intrusion comparator.
- **Why not admitted:** neither a non-overlapping natural carrier nor the independent comparator contract is frozen.  Changing the schedule is already a native policy surface, so it cannot itself be the contribution.
