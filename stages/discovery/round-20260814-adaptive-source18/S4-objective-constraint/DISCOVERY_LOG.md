## Why no deep review

Each fresh row has a real published result or result discussion, a versioned public carrier and a stock benchmark oracle.  None, however, concurrently freezes the remaining D1 obligations: an **independent** validity subtractor (not a different score/configuration of the same benchmark), confound-complete full-cost denominator, decision/ranking-change threshold and predeclared held-out falsifier.  Treating the results table itself as the subtractor would be circular; treating a benchmark setting as the correction would be a configuration selector.  Under `0 D1 → 0 depth`, no candidate-grade depth or outcome-aware backfill occurred.

The failure is an evidence-contract boundary, not an inference from implementation, result availability, hardware, resource or AI readiness.
