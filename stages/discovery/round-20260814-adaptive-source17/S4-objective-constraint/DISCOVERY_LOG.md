## D1 failure record

The nearest non-repeated evidence objects, SPECworkstation and SPECaccel, demonstrate why a result log cannot be promoted by itself.  A valid independent measurement/benchmark paper would need all of the following before depth: a release-pinned, multi-system/workload natural panel; a declared estimand; full confound fields (system/driver/firmware/power limit, ambient/thermal state, run rules and measurement device); a decision/ranking-change threshold; an **independent** validity subtractor; all collection/validation/analysis costs; and a predesignated held-out workload/system falsifier.  Their present public disclosure records do not close those fields.  The lack is not an inference from unavailable hardware, implementation, results, resources or AI readiness.
