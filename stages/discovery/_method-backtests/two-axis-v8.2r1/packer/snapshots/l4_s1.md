The Tail at Scale
Jeffrey Dean; Luiz André Barroso
Communications of the ACM, 56 (2013), pp. 74-80

VERBATIM_VISIBLE_ABSTRACT
It is challenging to keep the tail of the latency distribution low for interactive services as the size and complexity of the system scales up or as overall utilization increases. Temporary high latency episodes which are unimportant in moderate size systems may come to dominate overall service performance at large scale.
