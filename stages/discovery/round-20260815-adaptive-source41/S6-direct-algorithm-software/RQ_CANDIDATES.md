## AVR frame construction

whole frame representation construction exceed current frame analysis/lowering?
RQ variants: frame-size only, ABI-equivalent prologue/epilogue, and a complete
bytes/stack-state frontier.

## AVR shift expansion construction

behavior, could target-specific shift sequence construction be more than the
current expansion pass? Variants: single shift, adjacent shifts and complete
sequence construction.
