# Candidate-grade deep reviews

## Xapian Glass

First-party anchor: [Xapian core](https://github.com/xapian/xapian). Same-object page layouts can preserve stock postings/query semantics, but that alone does not prove a whole-constructor residual. Full current bulk-build configuration, B-tree construction papers and exact/FPT collision have not been frozen. Finite killer: pin source/options, generate two native legal layout witnesses from one corpus, replay union and stock queries, and reject if absorbed or if no charged build/query Pareto guarantee remains. `NOT_ADMITTED_UNFROZEN`.

## GNU gettext

First-party anchor: [GNU gettext](https://git.savannah.gnu.org/cgit/gettext.git). `.mo` reader equivalence permits a narrow representation witness, yet it is unknown whether current `msgfmt` paths and published minimal/perfect-hash and string-layout techniques already cover the proposed joint construction. Finite killer: pin source/options, build two reader-equivalent catalogs, replay union and lookups, then reject on absorption, changed lookup semantics or tuning-only residue. `NOT_ADMITTED_UNFROZEN`.

## Sphinx Search

First-party anchor: [Sphinx Search](https://github.com/sphinxsearch/sphinx). A fixed document/analyzer/query object admits possible layout variation, but complete builder controls and direct compressed-inverted-index collision have not been closed. Finite killer: pin source/options, two native indexes, stock query/digest equality and full index/query ledger. `NOT_ADMITTED_UNFROZEN`.
