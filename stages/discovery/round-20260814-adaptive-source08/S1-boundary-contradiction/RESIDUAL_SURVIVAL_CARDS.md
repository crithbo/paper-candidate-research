# Residual-survival cards

These cards are pre-depth hypotheses, not accepted candidates. A `D1` card has a concrete same-object two-action witness skeleton; it has not thereby survived the final v8.7 gate.

| Card | Signature | Fixed object / guarantee | Residual proposition | Current first-party sources | Witness skeleton | Controller/generic risk | Pre-depth class |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | `LLVM_DWARF_CALLSITE__PARAMETER_RECONSTRUCTIBILITY__REGALLOC_SPILL_DEBUG_EMISSION__FIXED_OPTIMIZED_CODE` | Optimized LLVM C/C++ MachineIR, same executable behavior and ABI, plus DWARF call-site parameter observability at a selected call. | Current debug-location propagation is after allocation; a budgeted joint choice of legal retained locations/rematerialization and call-site expressions may Pareto-improve reconstructibility without altering executable semantics. | [LLVM debug semantics](https://llvm.org/docs/SourceLevelDebugging.html), [instruction-reference design](https://llvm.org/docs/InstrRefDebugInfo.html), [current LDV source](https://llvm.org/doxygen/LiveDebugValues_8cpp_source.html). | One parameter is live only in a clobbered argument register at a call; legal alternatives are an entry value/undef versus a pre-call retained spill or legal rematerialization expression, all with identical machine result. | Moderate: must not reduce to generic RA or debug post-processing. | `D1_WITNESSABLE` |
| C02 | `GO_CGO_PINNER__FOREIGN_RETENTION__GC_PIN_LIFETIME__FIXED_CGO_POINTER_RULES` | Fixed Go↔C ownership trace and cgo pointer-safety contract. | A whole ownership interval construction might reduce pin-set churn while preserving valid foreign retention. | [cgo rules](https://go.dev/cmd/cgo/?m=old), [runtime Pinner source](https://go.dev/src/runtime/pinner.go), [cgocall source](https://go.dev/src/runtime/cgocall.go). | Two legal Pinner lifetimes cover the same foreign calls, but one coalesces intervals. | High: likely lifetime controller/batching. | `D2_RAW_ONLY` |
| C03 | `WASM_GC_RECTYPE__RECURSIVE_GROUP_PARTITION__VALIDATION_CANONICALIZATION__FIXED_WASM_MODULE` | Fixed Wasm GC module behavior, validation and nominal type-index semantics. | A recursive-type group construction might improve canonicalization/validation cost while retaining accepted module behavior. | [GC type specification](https://webassembly.github.io/gc/core/syntax/types.html), [Wasm GC draft](https://webassembly.github.io/gc/core/_download/WebAssembly.pdf), [Wasmtime upstream](https://github.com/bytecodealliance/wasmtime). | Two syntactically legal rec-group partitions for mutually recursive definitions; candidate checks whether they preserve type-index and subtyping observations. | Moderate: grouping may itself change semantics. | `D1_WITNESSABLE` |
| C04 | `LINUX_ROBUST_FUTEX__LIST_OP_PENDING__OWNER_DIED_PUBLICATION__FIXED_EXIT_RECOVERY` | Fixed robust mutex trace, thread exit and `FUTEX_OWNER_DIED` observable recovery. | List publication and kernel exit recovery expose a split state transition. | [official robust-futex documentation](https://docs.kernel.org/locking/robust-futexes.html), [Linux futex source](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/tree/master/kernel/futex), [glibc source](https://sourceware.org/git/?p=glibc.git). | Reorder list/list-op-pending updates while preserving crash windows. | High: correctness path is ABI-mandated; likely no free action. | `D2_RAW_ONLY` |
| C05 | `RISCV_ZACAS__CAS_VS_LRSC__ATOMIC_LOWERING_MEMORY_ORDER__FIXED_RISCV_ABI` | Fixed C/C++ atomic operation, memory order, RISC-V ABI and observable concurrency semantics. | Legal CAS and LR/SC forms impose different retry/code-size cost; perhaps a mixed whole-region lowering has a target-specific guarantee. | [RISC-V ISA manual](https://docs.riscv.org/reference/isa/_attachments/riscv-unprivileged.pdf), [LLVM RISC-V usage](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/blob/main/llvm/docs/RISCVUsage.rst), [LLVM upstream](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/tree/main/llvm/lib/Target/RISCV). | A fixed compare-exchange loop can use a Zacas form or LR/SC loop under the same memory-order contract. | Moderate/high: ordinary instruction selection is an obvious absorption risk. | `D1_WITNESSABLE` |
| C06 | `RISCV_ZABHA__BYTE_HALFWORD_AMO__LOWERING_ALIGNMENT_COST__FIXED_RISCV_MEMORY_SEMANTICS` | Fixed byte/halfword atomic program semantics and RISC-V target. | New AMO forms expose an alignment/form choice across atomic regions. | [RISC-V ISA manual](https://docs.riscv.org/reference/isa/_attachments/riscv-unprivileged.pdf), [LLVM RISC-V usage](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/blob/main/llvm/docs/RISCVUsage.rst), [GCC upstream](https://gcc.gnu.org/git/?p=gcc.git). | Byte/halfword operation may lower to extension form or emulation sequence. | High: target form selection/cost tuning. | `D2_RAW_ONLY` |
