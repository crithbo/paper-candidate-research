# Same-object RQ variants

Before evidence closure:

1. For a fixed RTL transition-system and fixed safety property, can a target-native proof-state update bound recourse after a local RTL edit while preserving the exact proof endpoint?
2. For the same object, can an edit-parameterized certificate bound be checked by the stock formal oracle?
3. For the same object, can public versioned RTL revisions yield a stable recourse characterization?

These are RQ versions, not families or claims.
