## Method and funnel

The batch used witness-first, subtractor-first screening. For every raw family a commit/visibility/recovery invariant was written before deep-review selection. Deep review then checked same-object semantics, current source/documented union (including configuration/API entrances), a minimal legal action-divergence witness, natural carrier, oracle, full-cost denominator, direct subtractor and a finite killer.
