## Residual gate

The retained proposal is not allowed to claim “global delta compression” or “better Git compression” generically. Before Stage A implementation, it must trace the actual current-union actions and demonstrate that candidate forest edges/layout choices lie outside that union while maintaining the exact self-contained-pack contract. Otherwise it terminates as `NO_FROZEN_RESIDUAL`, without treating missing results or AI readiness as scientific negative evidence.
