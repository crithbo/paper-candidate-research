### io_uring submission/completion ownership boundary

The Linux UAPI and current kernel source explicitly expose `IORING_SETUP_SINGLE_ISSUER`, cooperative/deferred task-run modes, SQ polling, no-SQ-array, registered rings, and constraints between them. The source enforces that deferred task running requires a single issuer and checks incompatible flag combinations. The 2025 original io_uring DBMS paper already studies registered buffers, passthrough I/O, buffer-manager and shuffle contexts, and reports a PostgreSQL case study.

The only action left by this scout is choosing/submitting among those native flags and batching policies.
