## Audit

- Direct collision is used only where source mechanisms cover the frozen exact object and action.
- The PalRUP route is deliberately not proposed despite public artifacts and a plausible CPU Stage A, because its residuals remain controllers/selectors rather than independent algorithms or representations.
- Therefore no candidate has either `structural_paper_potential >= TIER_B_Q2_VIABLE` or a corresponding `current_evidence_readiness`; both fields are `NOT_APPLICABLE__NO_RETAINED_CANDIDATE`, not low readiness.
