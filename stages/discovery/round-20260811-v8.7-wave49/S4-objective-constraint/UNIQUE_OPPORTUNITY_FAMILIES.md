# Wave49 unique opportunity families

## Family separation

`MDK-SLO-DENSITY`, `OBASE-OBJECT-TIERING`, `REAL-EMULATION-PARTITION`, and `PD3-DPU-PREFETCH` are candidate-grade because their O4 tension can be expressed as a possible joint action with a natural/formal carrier. They are not briefs: all require the current-source reality record below to freeze an admissible atomic action and fair union.

`SONIC-ORAM-EVICTION` is retained only as `NOT_ADMITTED_UNFROZEN`; the official source says its final paper remains embargoed, so no non-public details are inferred. The other six are structural drops explained in the collision matrix.
