## Method and scope

- Applied accepted production Discovery v2/v8.1, v8.6 fidelity-closure planning,
  and v8.7 current-upstream-reality discipline.  The TLS-blocked two-axis
  v8.2r2 method was not used.
- The screen starts from 2025–2026 first-party paper genealogies, then maps a
  claimed bottleneck transfer to an exact public-system object.  It excludes
  Wave29–44 objects, WebGraph, HNSW, UST, Knative, and active/blocked/terminal
  topics.  No experiment, benchmark, download, automation, downstream stage,
  or control-file change was made.
- Stage 0 is a conditional paper-potential gate.  Missing implementation,
  native output, positive result, resource, or AI readiness was not used as a
  negative criterion.  A direction is retained only when its *whole* action,
  same-function guarantee, strongest-union comparator, natural carrier, and
  finite fidelity closure can be frozen.

tested against the six residual dimensions.  The details and source routes are

the strongest union.  These are direct-mechanism or controller exclusions, not
readiness failures.
