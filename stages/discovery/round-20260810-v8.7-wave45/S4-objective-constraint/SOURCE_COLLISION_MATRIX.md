## Baseline and fidelity rules applied

- Unions are finite, compatible, same-object, same-information, and full-cost; they are not omniscient “anything-goes” unions.
- A current-source action is counted only when the cited official record explicitly describes it. No inference from a nondefault flag or hidden interface is made.
- Natural carriers are recorded only as evidence routes. Their availability/readiness has no role in the `DROP` decisions.
- Latest collision classification is `DIRECT_FATAL` only for Lockify's exact replay; all other drops are `DIRECT_SUBTRACT` or structurally uncrossable same-object failures, not a claim of global novelty exhaustion.
