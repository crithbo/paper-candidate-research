## Method and guardrails

This pass used the accepted, calibrated production Discovery method v2 / selection v8.1 only. The TLS-blocked two-axis v8.2r2 method was not used, claimed, or consulted. The research procedure followed **baseline-residual-first**:

1. Freeze a natural same-object stream, its update and query semantics, space/error contract, and full cost.
2. Name the strongest fair deployable baseline union before proposing a mechanism. Offline/static methods can be ceilings only.
3. Retain only an atomic N1/N2/N3 action outside that union; reject selectors, controllers, wrappers, checker work, ordinary tuning, and a generic request to “make it dynamic.”

The prohibited object families were excluded before ideation: dynamic sparse/KV, quantiles, online quantization, IPv6 LPM, checkers, controllers, future-work copying, and terminal-topic revival.

## Natural-input and finite-killer boundary

Natural data sources exist in principle for all inspected areas (repetitive text corpora, temporal graph updates, verification constraints, and event streams), but that fact alone is not a proposal. A Stage-A killer can be frozen only after there is a distinct mechanism and a fair union to falsify. For every surviving non-prohibited seed, at least one of those prerequisites was absent or directly absorbed. No 20–50-instance killer was therefore invented post hoc.

The work did **not** reject any idea for missing code, missing results, resource availability, or AI readiness. The zero result is solely an evidence-honest finding that no brief met the structural gates. It is bounded discovery, not a claim that the fields contain no remaining research problems.
