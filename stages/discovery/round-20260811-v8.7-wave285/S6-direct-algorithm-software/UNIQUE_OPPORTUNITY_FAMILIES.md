- Current union to close: Crossgen2 single/composite/partial-composite routes; section/table emission; profile/order/compression/determinism controls; runtime loader and R2RDump interpretation; every default and nondefault flag.
- Conditional N2: R2R-specific exact/FPT/Pareto/approximation/formal algorithm over the complete constructor with a full build/delta/load/startup cost guarantee. Generic PE reorder/delta/bin packing is excluded.
- Natural finite route: official runtime/app version pairs; R2RDump plus loader/runtime equality; crossgen/build CPU-RSS-temp, bytes/delta/apply, load/startup CPU-RSS ledger.
