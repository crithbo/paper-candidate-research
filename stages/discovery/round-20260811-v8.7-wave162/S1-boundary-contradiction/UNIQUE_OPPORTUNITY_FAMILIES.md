# UNIQUE_OPPORTUNITY_FAMILIES

Six deduplicated families were screened. Their common lens is a producer committing data/state/meaning which a downstream consumer must interpret or own without changing the declared object: GPU C/R; compiler profiling; binary decompilation; SBOM redistribution; GPU collective overlap; and failure recovery.

Natural carriers respectively are GCR C/R state traces; Neutrino's published kernel/probe traces; Bin2Wrong's source/compiler/format tuples; a software artifact with its SBOM redistribution path; collective-plus-kernel traces; and training failure/reconnect traces. Full cost always includes production, handoff/serialization, consumer processing, synchronization, validation/oracle, recovery/cleanup, and normal-path impact.

The R04/R05 interfaces could not be frozen to an enumerable current native action union from the bounded first-party source pass, and are admission failures rather than STOPs. R06 is a forbidden controller form. R01--R03 are candidate-grade diagnostic reviews below.
