# RQ candidates

## RQ01 — LoongArch TLS descriptor relocation / ABI construction

For a fixed LoongArch TLS-descriptor program, ABI, relocation endpoint and observable value, is there a target-specific joint construction of legal address/materialization/relocation forms that has a guarantee beyond ordinary form selection and linker relaxation?

## RQ02 — AMDGPU flat-scratch frame state

For a fixed AMDGPU kernel/call ABI and observable memory result, is there an AMDGPU-specific whole-function scratch/frame-state construction with a guarantee beyond current frame lowering, spill placement, and register allocation?
