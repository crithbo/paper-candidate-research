# Unique opportunity families

## TADY-CONSTRAINED-DISASSEMBLY

The only family examined is complete disassembly construction for a fixed binary: choose instruction boundaries, blocks, edges, and a structurally valid subset under scores/constraints. The red team rejects it under the frozen object because the available artifact's correctness target is labelled accuracy/coverage, not a native binary reader oracle for a unique complete output, and the remaining construction choices are covered by model/pruning configurations or become a model/postprocessor replacement.
