- Exact object: distributed graph analytics with unchanged graph-program semantics; objective is lower communication/memory cost while retaining correct execution.
- Same-information strong union: full mirroring/BSP, Pluto static partial mirroring, and Pluto mirror-free work migration under the graph and execution information described by the paper.
- Candidate-like action considered: dynamically changing remote-state materialization or migration from online graph progress.
- Disposition: this is a policy/selector over the anchor's data-placement and migration actions unless it introduces a new invariant-preserving representation.  No complete representation or finite witness was identified.  Recasting an offline/online placement choice as N2 would violate the controller/selector exclusion.

### FRACTAL — `DROP`

- Exact object: fault-tolerant distributed execution of unmodified shell scripts, preserving exactly-once recovery semantics downstream of side effects.

- Candidate-like action considered: changing recovery-region selection from future execution information.
- Disposition: an online region-selection rule is a controller over FRACTAL's recovery action space; a representation-changing construction was not specified without altering the semantics or relying on a generic oracle.  Therefore no action-gap certificate or finite same-object killer exists.

### SBB — `DROP`

- Same-information strong union: SBB user timer/NIC interrupts, flow migration for persistent imbalance, and task stealing for temporary imbalance under local runtime observations.

- Disposition: this is a scheduling policy substitution; no union-external complete algorithmic action, distinct guarantee, or natural finite falsifier was found.  A proposed parameter adaptation would be an explicitly prohibited controller.
