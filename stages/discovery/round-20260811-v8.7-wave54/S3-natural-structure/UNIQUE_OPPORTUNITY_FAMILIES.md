# Unique opportunity families and cross-lane boundary

Their in-progress research records were not read. This S3 package therefore restricts itself to natural graph/model structure and avoids their mechanism spaces, as well as Wave51 eBPF/JVM/DWARF near-threshold families, pure emitters and online controllers.
