## Bounded-search limitations

- This matrix does not establish universal nonexistence. It establishes that, at the 2026-08-09 cutoff and within the stated direct-algorithm formulations, no residual cleared the Q2 hard gate.
- No negative conclusion is based on unavailable equipment, download failure, missing proprietary trace, or AI readiness.
- No prior terminal candidate was reopened, renamed, or used as a baseline-shaped substitute.
