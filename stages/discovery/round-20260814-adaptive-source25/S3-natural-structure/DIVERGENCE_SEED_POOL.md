## Perspective rotation and coverage selection

The pool rotates end-user, maintainer, compiler-backend, operator, measurement-reviewer, theorist, and hardware-architect perspectives.  Clustering produced: (1) temporal public graph, (2) geospatial history, (3) package metadata, (4) excluded/saturated or restricted scopes.  To cover distinct admissible clusters without forcing a Top-N, locators S01–S04 were selected.  S05–S08 remain a non-evidentiary backlog; S09–S11 are excluded before locator work; S12 is a shared conclusion-first falsifier.

No seed was promoted by score.  A locator could enter raw only after its Question Card closed the exact identity, same-object estimand, non-generic consequence, current union, contrary/subtractor, minimum falsifier, finite route, and initial full-cost denominator.
