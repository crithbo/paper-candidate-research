## Carry-forward closure decision

The frozen ROCprofiler action tension remains technically real: dispatch counting serializes kernel execution and can deadlock co-dependent kernels; device counting keeps device-wide execution but does not attach counters to individual kernels.  Current official HIP/hipFile sources also demonstrate legal asynchronous multi-stream behavior and an equality witness.  But the available ROCm examples are explicitly examples/tutorial artifacts, not the required versioned natural cross-application panel.  Substituting them for natural evidence would change the contribution's evidence object.  This finite closure therefore fails closed as `NOT_ADMITTED_UNFROZEN`; it is not extended into another ROCprofiler assignment.  Missing hardware, implementation, results, resources or AI readiness were not used as negative evidence.
