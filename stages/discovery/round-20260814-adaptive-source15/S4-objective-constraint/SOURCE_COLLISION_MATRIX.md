## ROCprofiler reality check and finite falsifier

Current ROCprofiler profiles are agent-specific and immutable; dispatch counting permits one hardware kernel at a time, while device counting observes a time range without kernel attribution.  Current HIP documentation says multiple streams may overlap but does not guarantee a natural workload population.  hipFile's multistream registered example preserves output through hash equality and documents a CPU-bounce fallback, yet is an official tutorial/example.  The planned held-out falsifier—whether a pre-registered natural concurrent application preserves a measurement conclusion across unprofiled/device/dispatch modes—cannot be constructed without first freezing that natural panel.  The failure is therefore evidentiary-contract incompleteness, not a scientific or resource STOP.
