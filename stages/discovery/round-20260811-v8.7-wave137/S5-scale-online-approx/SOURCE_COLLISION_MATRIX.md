## Cross-lane/repeat check

The matrix deliberately excludes Wave54's Pluto/FRACTAL/SBB, Wave63 streaming-summary reconfiguration, Waves73–129 migration/rebuild/recourse objects, and earlier dynamic sparse/KV/quantile/online-quantization work. The surviving entries are corpus-characterization objects, not those constructors. No active or terminal registry object was reused as a proposed brief; GPU-v2023 is explicitly screened out because the source already supplies its named FGD mechanism.
