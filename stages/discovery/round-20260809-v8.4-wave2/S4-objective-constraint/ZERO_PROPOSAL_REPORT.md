# Wave 2 zero-proposal report

## Decision

No `PROPOSE_STAGE0` recommendation is made.

Each surveyed line has credible structural paper potential at field level, and several have public natural workloads.  That does **not** satisfy the requirement for a new candidate.  This wave found no exact object with a new N1/N2/N3 action that remains after comparison with current strong mechanisms.

| Lead family | Structural paper potential | Current evidence readiness | Candidate disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dynamic-compilation IR reuse / code cache | High as a field; a future representation-level result could be Q1/Q2 shaped. | Existing 2025 paper and natural JVM suites are available. | Not a candidate: only a cache-policy variant was identifiable. |
| Mobile binary outlining | High as a field; production-app deployment has a clear capacity objective. | Current paper supplies an implementation-oriented anchor. | Not a candidate: choice under a budget is absorbed by the existing outlining/selection action. |
| Application-integrated far memory | High as a systems field; local capacity changes the problem materially. | Natural example applications and current designs are public. | Not a candidate: no new same-semantics representation or non-policy algorithm was specified. |
| ORAM / block-store tail latency | High as fields. | Current mechanisms exist; SONIC's full paper was embargoed at inspection. | Not candidates: direct collision for ORAM; prohibited controller form for block storage. |

## Why no finite Stage A killer is attached

There are no candidates.  A finite Stage A killer is mandatory only once an exact object and a distinct mechanism are frozen.  Here, the earliest honest killer would simply demonstrate that a policy/parameterization is absorbed by an incumbent.  The lane therefore abstains before creating a misleading experiment route.

## Re-entry condition for a later, new assignment

A future S4 wave may revisit any family only with a newly specified exact object that:

1. changes a representation, algorithm, information condition, or guarantee—not a budget/priority/eviction selector;
2. keeps function, quality/security semantics, and full CPU/memory/I/O/compile/recovery cost comparable;
3. has named current same-object baselines and a public natural workload; and
4. admits a finite CPU/solver killer that can reject the mechanism against those baselines.

This is not a reservation, revival, or pre-registration of any topic.
