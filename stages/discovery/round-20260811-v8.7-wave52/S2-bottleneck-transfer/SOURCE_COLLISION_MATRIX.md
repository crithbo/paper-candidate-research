|---|---|---|---|

## Candidate-grade admission boundary

An opportunity could enter deep review only after the action is expressed as a
complete transformation on the same final object, with an exact guarantee,
current source/action inventory (including defaults and opt-ins), a natural or
formal-natural carrier, complete denominator, and finite fidelity closure.
None of the eight unique raw families satisfies that boundary.  Therefore no
latest-paper collision claim, source-absence claim, or 72-hour Stage-A killer
is manufactured.
