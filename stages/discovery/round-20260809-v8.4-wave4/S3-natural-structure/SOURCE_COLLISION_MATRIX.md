## Audit discipline

- Each comparison is same-object and preserves output semantics.  No cross-layer or cross-workload speedup is used as evidence.
- The named sources are current as of the assignment cutoff.  `SEARCH_BOUNDED_OPEN` is not used as a newness claim.
- “No experimental claim” means neither success nor failure was inferred from lack of a local run.  The Stage-A concepts are recorded only to show that a retained mechanism would have been finitely falsifiable.
- No row is a grounded brief because all fail before the template can honestly freeze structural-paper potential at `TIER_B_Q2_VIABLE`, an exact residual contribution, a fair baseline union, and a finite killer together.
