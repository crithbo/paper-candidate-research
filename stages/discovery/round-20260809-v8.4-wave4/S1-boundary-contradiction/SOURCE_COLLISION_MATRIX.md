## AMD terminology and target-mapping audit

The only claims retained here are documentary facts: rocWMMA exposes `gfx1250` with wave32 support, and LLVM’s AMDGPU target table labels `gfx1250` as an APU. This matrix **does not** equate `gfx1250` with CDNA5, infer a complete ISA, or equate AMD Tensor Data Mover/Tensor DMA descriptors with NVIDIA TMA. A complete architecture disclosure, ISA definition, and toolchain mapping are independent requirements; none is substituted for another.

## Conclusion

No lead leaves a new same-object N1/N2/N3 mechanism with current strong baselines, natural evidence, a complete cost ledger, reproducible route, and finite Stage A falsifier. This is a zero-proposal result, not a claim that missing AMD public material implies scientific failure.
