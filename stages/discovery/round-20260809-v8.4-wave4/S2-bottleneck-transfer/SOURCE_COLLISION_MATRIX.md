## Collision and quality conclusion

- `DIRECT_FATAL` is applied only to the first and fourth rows, where exact mechanism coverage is explicit.
- The zkVM and SP1 rows are dropped without claiming an external direct paper collision: their failure is structural—routine tuning or object drift.
- No weak union is used as a baseline: any later new-object proposal would need a deployable same-stack baseline union, a full-cost contract (compile, host execution, proving/verification and data movement as applicable), and a natural public trace/program corpus.
