### 1. Source-unit qualification

A raw problem lead remains admissible when it has:

1. an exact current object/interface and invariant guarantee;
2. a concrete problem anchor from a recent original work, versioned artifact behavior, measurement validity conflict or formal boundary;
3. a reachable current first-party documentation/source route;
4. no known repeat, active/hold identity conflict, terminal STOP revival or assignment exclusion.

Complete union closure, proof of novelty, implementation and positive results are not raw prerequisites. Missing them cannot by itself cause structural DROP.

### 3. Native-action fingerprint for GPU/NPU compiler and architecture rows

Before depth, list the current union's action coordinates, such as layout, tile, precision, calibration, memory mode, pipeline depth, barrier/wait, engine/library selection, pass order and scheduling policy. Then compare the proposed residual:

- if it only selects, reweights, orders or searches the same coordinates, classify `EARLY_STRUCTURAL_RISK__SELECTOR_OR_CURRENT_UNION`;
- if it only instantiates a target-independent scheduling/layout/packing/ISel kernel, classify `EARLY_STRUCTURAL_RISK__GENERIC_KERNEL`;
- rank as `D1_WITNESSABLE` only when there is a same-guarantee witness plus a plausible target-specific state coupling, constraint, correction, certificate or complexity residual not already represented by the fingerprint.

This is not a direct-collision verdict. A structural DROP still requires the bounded current-source/contrary evidence required by v8.7, and Stage0 must independently reconstruct the union.

### 4. Source priorities for the next boundary

Prefer, without quotas:

- recent original papers whose explicit limitation can be checked against a pinned current source/object, rather than limitation text alone;
- IR/ABI/ISA/runtime seams with two complete legal plans under unchanged ownership, lifetime, numerical and synchronization guarantees;
- GPU/NPU cross-layer mechanisms where native instruction/memory/collective semantics create target-specific bounded state, not merely a scheduler flag grid;
- checksum-pinned model/compiler/kernel version histories with a real natural carrier before natural-law depth;
- measurement validity conflicts where the instrument changes observability or the measured object, paired with a versioned benchmark/workload contract;
- public stock semantic checkers or executable same-contract oracles; proprietary opaque NPU constructors without such a route remain recorded but rank below scarce depth.

Do not backfill unused source slots with mature caches, ordinary constructors, option grids, dashboard/reporting or generic formal wrappers.

### 5. Measurement/benchmark independent lane behavior

Keep S4 independent. For measurement/benchmark depth, require before selection:

- a versioned public natural workload/submission panel;
- predeclared estimand and conclusion-changing threshold;
- collection/sampling/thermal/cache/clock confounds;
- current fair measurement union;
- collection-to-analysis full cost;
- held-out falsifier.

A new algorithmic action is not required. A tool flag, mode selector, dashboard, metric restatement or consequence-free dataset remains insufficient.

### 6. Depth capacity and chronology

1. Freeze every raw card and the depth ranking before any depth result.
2. Use at most two `D1_WITNESSABLE` rows per lane normally.
3. A third depth is allowed only if at least three cards were already `D1_WITNESSABLE`, evidence-role complete or explicitly one-step complete, and ranked before outcomes.
4. Never backfill a third slot after the first two fail; never promote an `R0` merely to fill capacity.
5. Each depth may answer at most the three predeclared decisive source questions. Additional literature requires a newly logged unresolved facet.

### 7. Bounded carry-forward and dormant scheduling

- An initial `NOT_ADMITTED_UNFROZEN` family may receive at most one pre-registered source-only carry-forward if it has one or two explicit missing facets, accessible sources, unchanged object/guarantee and no known direct absorption.
- Across a cycle, retain the R17 ceiling of at most four carry-forwards total and at most one per lane. Unfilled capacity stays unfilled.
- If the carry-forward ends with the same decisive missing facet, assign local scheduling label `DORMANT_SOURCE_SATURATED_UNFROZEN` for at least three complete six-lane cycles.
- The label is not a registry candidate state, STOP, reserve or scientific conclusion. It only prevents repeated Discovery expenditure.
- Re-entry requires one documented external delta: new first-party release/commit, new original paper/artifact, newly public oracle/natural carrier, or explicit user/mainline targeted authorization. A changed object or research question receives a new family identity.
- Source15's ROCprofiler carry-forward is already the one allowed retry. If its frozen finite route fails, it must not receive another routine carry-forward under this pilot.

## Discovery versus Stage0 boundary

Discovery supplies an attackable proposition, not an exhaustive novelty verdict. It must freeze the card, bounded current-source reality, witness skeleton, comparator classes, carrier/oracle route, full-cost axes and fidelity closure plan. Stage0 independently performs the complete same-object/current-union/genealogy/Q calibration and decides whether the residual is Tier B/Q1-shaped.

No card field requires implementation, proof, hardware result or a successful main claim. No card may turn resource absence into a scientific negative.
