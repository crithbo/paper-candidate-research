## Problem statement

R16A/R16B excluded 34 of 82 observed family-graph inputs as repeat/contained/cross-source relationships, and their 48 accepted families split into 23 structural drops and 25 unfrozen cases with zero clean brief. canonical-resume01 independently produced zero briefs under v8.7. The process is spending substantial breadth and depth on saturated mature artifact constructors and closing current unions only after deep review, while finite near-miss closure and measurement/formal source lines receive less targeted attention.

## Current rule

- `AGENTS.md` and `ROLE_DISCOVERY.md` already authorize adaptive source distribution after two zero-clean-brief cycles and require V9 to remain nonproduction.
- adaptive-source01 already shifts the six lanes toward measurement, formal, compiler/architecture semantics, cross-layer algorithms and current-source-verifiable limitations.
- The remaining gap is operational: no cross-cycle saturation ledger, no explicit aggregate source budget, no carry-forward near-miss closure allocation, and no one-to-one failure-facet telemetry.

## Proposed minimum delta: next-assignment pilot only

Mainline may freeze these fields in the next safe six-lane assignment. They rank source effort; they do not change `PROPOSE_STAGE0` or `DROP` semantics.

### 1. Source-archetype reference mix

For a reference cycle of 36 raw family slots, use mutually exclusive primary source labels and scale proportionally if the actual cycle is smaller or larger:

| Primary source archetype | Reference slots | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Measurement/characterization and natural N3 laws | 10 | Ranking reversals, evaluation bias, stable workload regimes, benchmark corrections |
| Target-specific formal/online/approximation guarantees | 7 | Theorem/certificate route tied to a real object |
| Compiler/runtime/ISA/ABI/architecture semantics | 7 | Cross-stage invariants and legal action divergence |
| Cross-layer joint algorithms | 7 | Two or more interacting layers with one full-cost objective |
| Recent explicit limitation + current-source verified residual | 5 | Paper/artifact/current-source triangulation |

These are source-effort targets, not proposal or acceptance quotas. An unfilled category remains unfilled; it must not be backfilled with a saturated cache/index family.

Cross-cutting cap for the pilot: mature cache/index/catalog/maintenance/ordinary-constructor archetypes receive at most 15% of raw source effort and at most one candidate-grade deep review per lane, unless the breadth record names a genuinely new action class and a primary current-source divergence witness. This cap expires after the pilot unless separately approved.

### 2. Strategic source triad before scarce deep review

Rank a new family for candidate-grade depth only after it has, or has a finite one-step route to:

1. a primary opportunity anchor: recent paper result/limitation, measurement anomaly, theorem boundary or current artifact behavior;
2. current first-party reality: official docs plus actual current source/default and nondefault action surface when absence matters;
3. strongest contrary/subtractor: same-object recent method, current native union, or generic/formal kernel.

Breadth rows may remain incomplete. Missing triad fields rank a row down; they do not create a scientific DROP.

### 3. Action-promise card

Before depth allocation, record a compact, nonbinding card:

- exact object and observable guarantee;
- two legal, non-synonymous actions or one explicit action-divergence witness;
- why the residual is not configuration/controller/wrapper/generic packing;
- intended contribution type and target-specific claim shape;
- natural/formal carrier and decisive falsifier;
- full-cost denominator.

This is a source-prioritization artifact, not proof of novelty or a requirement for implementation/results.

### 4. Carry-forward finite near-miss closure

Across the six lanes, reserve capacity for at most four previously `NOT_ADMITTED_UNFROZEN` families whose missing facets are finite and source-only. They remain the same opportunity-family identity and are counted as rescreens, not new families. Select them before outcomes using:

- one or two explicit missing facets;
- current-source access available;
- named action/Q2 route;
- diversity across source archetypes;
- no known direct absorption.

Suggested initial closure pool from canonical-resume01: OpenMP libomptarget lifetime, DuckDB checkpoint action, one measurement/natural-law candidate, and one target-specific formal/algorithmic candidate such as eBPF verifier state. This list is illustrative; mainline must re-freeze exact source boundaries and may choose fewer or none.

### 5. Measurement-characterization alignment

For `MEASUREMENT_CHARACTERIZATION` or `BENCHMARK_DATASET`, do not require a new exploitation algorithm at Discovery if the package instead freezes:

- a natural, confound-controlled observable;
- a stable law, ranking reversal, evaluation bias or benchmark coverage error;
- a decision-changing consequence;
- current measurement baselines and contrary evidence;
- a reproducible collection/analysis plan and claim ceiling.

This applies the existing typed contribution contract. Dashboard-only, synthetic-only and consequence-free measurement still fails Q2 shape.

### 6. Saturation and failure telemetry

Each raw row receives one primary disposition and any number of secondary facets. Required primary reasons:

- `EXCLUDED_REPEAT_OR_CONTAINED`
- `DROP_SAME_OBJECT`
- `DROP_CURRENT_UNION_DIRECT`
- `DROP_GENERIC_OR_CONTROLLER_REMAINDER`
- `NOT_ADMITTED_ACTION_UNCLOSED`
- `NOT_ADMITTED_CURRENT_UNION_UNCLOSED`
- `NOT_ADMITTED_NATURAL_CARRIER_OR_LAW`
- `NOT_ADMITTED_Q2_SHAPE`
- `NOT_ADMITTED_SOURCE_PIN`
- `PROPOSE_STAGE0`

Record raw event, unique family, rescreen/carry-forward, deep review and clean brief as separate units. The cycle handoff must mechanically reconcile every raw row; this prevents the one-row gap observed in S1 canonical-resume01.

## Safety invariants

- Q2 minimum and Q1 priority: unchanged.
- Same-object/latest collision/fair current strong union: strengthened earlier in source selection, not relaxed.
- Natural evidence/full-cost/reproducibility/claim ceiling: unchanged.
- Missing implementation or result: still not a DROP reason.
- STOP: never revived; carry-forward applies only to nonterminal `NOT_ADMITTED_UNFROZEN`.
- Proposal quota: none.
- Parallelism: six active lanes remain the ceiling; no new lane is proposed.
- V9: remains `OFF`.
- Stage0/A/B/Production: not authorized by this proposal.

1. repeat/contained exclusion burden;
