# Two-slot selective strong-model depth

Apply only when the frozen six-lane assignment enables this profile. The six breadth lanes remain `gpt-5.6-terra / high`; the cycle may use at most two `gpt-5.6-sol / high` one-level subagents.

## Nomination barrier

Each lane may nominate at most one pre-RQ closure packet. Freeze every nomination before reading any strong-model result. Select packets only after all six lanes have either nominated or declared none, so lane speed cannot capture both slots.

## Mechanical eligibility

A packet is eligible only when it has:

1. one exact public anchor and version/date;
2. a stable object and immutable semantic/quality envelope;
3. one decision-relevant counterfactual consequence;
4. one answerable anchor question whose answer jointly defines a bounded `carrier/action/estimand` bundle;
5. one or at most two named primary sources and a finite stop condition;
6. no already-known direct fatal, terminal exact identity, changed object/guarantee, or forbidden scope.

Do not require the final exact RQ or complete typed tuple before eligibility; closing that tuple is the packet's job.

## Result-independent selection

Mainline selects at most two packets using this order:

1. mechanically eligible over ineligible;
2. one-source closure over two-source closure;
3. a missing bundle that directly controls raw admission over optional enrichment;
4. portfolio diversity in object and contribution type;
5. frozen nomination timestamp as the final tie-break.

Do not inspect scientific outcomes, model confidence, or likely positive results when selecting. Do not backfill after seeing a selected packet's result.

## Strong-model task

The strong model may only:

- freeze exact carrier, atomic action, and estimand from the named sources;
- find the most obvious generic solver/current-union/strong-baseline objection;
- determine whether a finite oracle, witness, checker, or falsifier route exists;
- return `CLOSED_TO_TYPED_RQ`, `EARLY_FATAL`, or `UNRESOLVED_BACKLOG` with evidence locators.

It must not replace the anchor, change object/guarantee/contribution type, invent a new topic, use outcome-aware reformulation, claim novelty/Q2, decide a clean brief, or substitute for Stage 0.

## Stop and rollback

Stop the upgrade after one prospective cycle if both used packets merely restate input, drift objects, cite no decisive primary material, or close nothing. Also stop if no packet is eligible after the relaxed pre-RQ eligibility defined here; diagnose source entry instead of widening the slots. Do not expand beyond two slots, add lanes, or increase retrieval budgets without new user approval.
