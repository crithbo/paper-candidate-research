# Research Topic Discovery

Turn broad research directions and public evidence into falsifiable, auditable Stage 0 briefs. Optimize investigation order and model effort; never compensate for or weaken the project's scientific gates.

## Establish authority and state

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `plan.md`, `registry.yaml`, `rules/ROLE_DISCOVERY.md`, the frozen assignment, and the three Discovery templates named by the role file.
2. Treat those files as authoritative. This skill is an operational runbook; it cannot change status, budgets, modes, scientific thresholds, or shared files.
3. Freeze the assignment's method revision, model/reasoning, lookup surface, unique write directory, resource policy hash, and `RESOURCE_ROOT` before work.
4. If the assignment does not explicitly freeze this skill's version/hash, fail closed and ask mainline for a corrected assignment.

## Run the funnel in order

### 1. Build a problem-first portfolio

- Generate a bounded seed pool before targeted evidence lookup. Use source-lane modes as viewpoints, not admission silos.
- Prefer unresolved decisions, measurable contradictions, target-specific semantics, formal guarantees, stable natural/canonical structure, and recent source-verifiable limitations.
- Do not categorically exclude a mature project or topic family. Exclude before evidence only for forbidden scope, literal/exact terminal identity, frozen direct collision, changed object/guarantee by definition, or a generic shell.
- Treat ideation engines and perspective coverage as advisory. Do not manufacture filler to satisfy a count.

Read [source-portfolios.md](references/source-portfolios.md) when selecting seeds, query roles, or lane emphasis.

### 2. Commit an anchor before deep search

For every selected seed, freeze one exact public anchor such as a paper, project/version, dataset, benchmark, specification, source locus, or canonical formal family. Record the tentative object and immutable semantic/quality boundary, one decision or conclusion that could change, one primary anchor question, and the precommitted primary source route plus transport-only fallback.

An anchor is a locator, not evidence of novelty, Q2 quality, or current absence.

### 3. Converge the research question

- Write one primary answerable RQ. Add up to two same-object alternatives only when genuine scope ambiguity remains; do not require three rewrites mechanically.
- Keep object, guarantee, contribution type, and information contract stable across alternatives.
- Use FINER-lite only for answerability, importance, scope, and scheduling.
- Do not require exact carrier, complete action, complete estimand, full current union, or final falsifier before allowing bounded source closure.

### 4. Route pre-RQ source closure

When an exact anchor exists but the final `carrier / atomic action / estimand` tuple is jointly defined by material not yet read, create one bounded closure packet instead of returning `LOCATOR_ONLY` merely because the Question Card is incomplete.

The packet must freeze the exact anchor and object/guarantee envelope, one outcome-independent question answerable from one or at most two named primary sources, the co-defined field bundle to close, no known direct fatal, finite stop conditions, and one of `CLOSED_TO_TYPED_RQ / EARLY_FATAL / UNRESOLVED_BACKLOG`.

Do not combine unrelated artifacts or objects merely to close several fields at once.

### 5. Use at most two strong-model packets

Read [selective-depth.md](references/selective-depth.md) whenever a full six-lane cycle enables selective depth. Strong-model work closes or rejects a frozen packet; it cannot invent a replacement topic.

### 6. Apply the evidence-qualified raw gate

Only after ordinary or selective source closure, complete `templates/DISCOVERY_QUESTION_CARD.md`. Require the authoritative raw fields: exact public identity, same-object contract, natural/canonical/implementation carrier kind, typed claim endpoint, counterfactual consequence, source-supported non-generic discriminator, one current source/spec locus, minimum falsifier, finite closure route, and initial full-cost boundary.

- Known direct fatal, changed object/guarantee, generic wrapper/solver only, or no finite auditable route cannot enter raw.
- Missing complete current union, strongest collision, full genealogy, full witness/interface/corpus, main result, or formal Q1/Q2 calibration may remain bounded debt owned by Discovery deep review, Stage 0, or Stage A preclaim as the role rule permits.
- Never interpret `NO_MATCH` as proof of novelty or absence.

### 7. Deep-review by contribution type

Use the cheapest type-appropriate anti-collapse check:

- method/compiler/system: atomic action survival plus target-specific decision consequence;
- measurement/characterization: exact estimand, observable carrier, confound route, and decision threshold;
- benchmark/dataset: validity or coverage endpoint and the conclusion it changes;
- theory/formal: fixed guarantee, canonical instance family, oracle/checker, and non-generic construction skeleton;
- replication/negative: exact claim, faithful same-object reproduction route, and decision-relevant negative endpoint.

Do not impose a universal non-product-coupling witness or action-enumeration trace on every contribution type. Do not make Discovery reconstruct the complete Stage 0 collision/Q2 review.

### 8. Build the brief or abstain honestly

Before `CLEAN_STAGE0_BRIEF`, assemble source roles rather than a paper-count quota: current anchor, necessary genealogy, strongest current/recent comparator, contrary or generic-kernel evidence, and a finite falsifier/oracle route. Report search boundaries and unavailable roles honestly.

Return only the dispositions authorized by `ROLE_DISCOVERY.md`. Zero briefs is legitimate. Missing implementation or main results is not a scientific failure when the frozen fidelity route is finite.

## Preserve telemetry without turning it into gates

Record seed, RQ, closure-packet, raw, deep, and brief denominators separately. Attribute each query or source action to its role so later audits can distinguish poor source yield from healthy scientific abstention. Do not rank by model confidence, venue prestige, paper count, or a compensatory score.

## Avoid rule churn

- Treat one-cycle profiles, shadows, and repair checks as expired unless the frozen assignment explicitly activates them.
- Do not revive fixed S3/S4 strong slots, universal non-product checks, broad mature-surface cooldown, outcome-aware backfill, or proposal quotas.
- After zero-yield cycles, diagnose the first failing transition and test one bounded change. Do not stack another micro-rule without retiring, retaining, or downgrading the previous one from prospective evidence.

## Handoff

Write the canonical handoff and manifest named by the role rule. State what was screened, where the funnel closed, whether either strong slot ran, what it materially closed or rejected, remaining bounded debt, and why any abstention is scientific, evidentiary, or mechanical. Notify mainline only at the role-defined boundaries.
