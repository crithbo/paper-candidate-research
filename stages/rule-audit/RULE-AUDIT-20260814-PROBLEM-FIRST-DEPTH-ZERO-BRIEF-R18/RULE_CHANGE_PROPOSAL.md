### Raw admission

A row may enter raw when all are present:

1. a concrete primary problem/result/measurement/theorem boundary;
2. an exact current object/interface and intended invariant guarantee;
3. an available current first-party route;
4. no already-known identity repeat, terminal STOP revival or explicit assignment exclusion.

Complete current-union closure, a proof of novelty, implementation and positive results are not raw prerequisites.

### Depth selection and capacity

1. Freeze all raw cards and a deterministic lexicographic ranking before any selected depth outcome is known.
2. Rank first by invariant object/guarantee survival, then witness concreteness, then bounded source-closure path, then absence of named controller/generic risk. Do not use model self-confidence or venue prestige.
3. Select at most two `D1_WITNESSABLE` cards normally.
4. A third card is allowed only when at least three cards were already classified `D1_WITNESSABLE` and pre-ranked before depth. Do not backfill with `R0` or choose the third after seeing whether the first two would produce briefs.
5. Each selected depth closes only the named current-source loci, strongest contrary and three decisive questions. Broader literature expansion requires a new bounded reason.
6. A row that remains uncertain returns `NOT_ADMITTED_UNFROZEN`; a structural drop still requires actual same-object/current-union/generic/guarantee evidence.

### Literature roles

For selected depth, use a strategic minimum set rather than undirected volume:

- opportunity/problem anchor;
- current first-party implementation and controls;
- strongest same-object or generic subtractor;
- one boundary/contrary source when the first three do not settle the residual.

More papers are justified only by an unresolved decisive facet, not by a target count.

## Discovery/Stage0 separation

Discovery must provide an exact attackable proposition, bounded current-source check, witness skeleton, named comparator classes, natural/formal carrier, full-cost axes and finite fidelity closure plan. It must not:

- declare exhaustive novelty;
- perform Stage0's independent eight-predicate direct-coverage decision;
- choose Q1/Q2 comparators or issue the final tier judgment;
- require implementation, proof or positive results;
- design/execute Stage A.

Stage0 independently reconstructs the decisive current union and genealogy, calibrates Tier A/B, judges collision and mechanism, and sets any bounded revision/Stage A killer.
