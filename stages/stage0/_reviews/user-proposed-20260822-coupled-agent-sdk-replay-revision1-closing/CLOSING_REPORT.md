## Strongest counter-argument and rejection

The strongest possible disagreement is that `KEEP_SHARED / SPLIT / INDEPENDENT_FALLBACK / UNCOMPARABLE / ABORT_PAIR` forms a target-specific adaptive agent-transition algorithm. Revision1 does not support that reading:

1. It gives no nontrivial policy choosing among those labels from observable frozen SDK state. The labels are legality/safety classifications.
2. `KEEP_SHARED` is restricted to pre-certified immutable equality; `SPLIT` is ordinary copy/reset; `FALLBACK` is the independent baseline; `UNCOMPARABLE` is worst-case accounting; `ABORT_PAIR` is pre-admission refusal.
3. None of the labels constrains the sign or magnitude of terminal covariance, retained-pair rate, sample complexity or full cost.
4. The all-fallback region is independent execution plus overhead. Early divergence permits zero or negative covariance. Those are generic coupled-estimator facts, not an SDK-specific frontier.

Thus the packet contains a useful engineering safety checklist, but not the N2 algorithmic residual required for a Tier B paper.
