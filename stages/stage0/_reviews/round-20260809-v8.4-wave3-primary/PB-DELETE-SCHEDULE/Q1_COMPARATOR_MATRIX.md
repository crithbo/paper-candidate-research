## Reviewer-shape assessment

- Editor/venue-fit view: a narrow, rigorous SAT/CP or formal-methods algorithm paper is credible when it preserves an unusually strong end-to-end output contract.
- Methodology view: checker acceptance is necessary but not enough; same output hash, full-cost and independent invariant review are required.
- Domain-contribution view: checked deletion is already known. The sole contribution must be the cost-aware legal construction algorithm.
- Devil's-advocate view: a fixed preprocessor trace may leave no freedom, in which case the candidate dies; the pre-registered 20% occurrence threshold makes this falsifiable.

- Quality tier: `TIER_B_Q2_VIABLE`
