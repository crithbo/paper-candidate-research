First-party current source independently supports this pipeline characterization. In BvCompZ, `reference_costs` records native estimated bit costs for root and legal reference deltas; `update_references_for_max_length` computes a bounded-depth subforest of the initial maximum forest; `find_additional_references_greedily` explicitly adds valid alternatives not in that forest; and `write_and_clear_current_chunk` uses the selected references in the native encoder. See the [current BvCompZ source](https://docs.rs/webgraph/latest/src/webgraph/graphs/bvgraph/comp/bvcompz.rs.html) and the [official WebGraph Rust repository](https://github.com/vigna/webgraph-rs).

## 4. Atomic mechanism: definable, but not yet empirically closed

The frozen revision itself contains a bounded-window frontier-DP/FPT residual. After removing the absorbed broad claim, a same-object static proposition can be stated without changing the graph, format, decoder, random-access contract, or legal reference actions:

> For a fixed ordered immutable graph, fixed native BVGraph coding flags, chunking, backward window `W`, and maximum reference depth `D`, obtain the exact native estimated bit cost `c(i,δ)` for the root action `δ=0` and every legal predecessor `1≤δ≤min(W,i)`. Select exactly one action per successor list so that every reference chain has depth at most `D`, minimizing the total native action cost. Emit the selected forest through the unchanged native writer, offsets, and reader.

This is `same-object`: the input remains exact successor lists; actions are the native root/reference alternatives; semantics remain lossless BVGraph serialization plus native random access; and the objective uses the same per-action native cost estimator rather than injected weights.

A finite exact algorithmic route is also definable. For fixed `W,D`, scan lists left to right with a frontier state containing the depths of the most recent `W` lists (plus deterministic reconstruction backpointers). At list `i`, transition to root depth zero or to any legal predecessor whose depth is below `D`, add the corresponding native cost `c(i,δ)`, and shift the frontier. This yields an exact shortest-path/dynamic-programming formulation with finite running time of the form `O(n·f(W,D)·W)`; a direct depth-vector construction gives an elementary upper bound with `f(W,D)≤(D+1)^W`, before state reduction. The guarantee is exact optimality over the frozen complete legal-action space, not optimality over a proxy forest.

That route still needs careful closure of chunk-boundary semantics, empty-reference legality, tie-breaking, cost-estimator/writer equality, and a formal recurrence/proof. Those are finite fidelity and methodology obligations. They are not evidence that the atomic mechanism cannot be defined.

## 6. Strongest devil's-advocate case and finite kill route

The candidate is still fragile. The strongest adverse case is:

- native BVGraph action costs may never instantiate a graph on which the exact complete-edge optimizer improves over final BvCompZ;
- even if a tiny divergence exists, the depth-vector DP may be a generic finite-state optimizer with no practically useful residual or sufficiently distinctive N2 contribution;
- exponential dependence on `W` may make the exact method useful only as a small oracle, not as a deployable compressor;
- any bit saving may disappear after selection overhead, offsets, build time, peak memory, and random-access/decode costs are fully counted.

These are legitimate scientific kill conditions, but the frozen review contains no native counterexample, equivalence proof, or full-cost result establishing them. The finite pre-claim closure is:

1. pin the BvCompZ source/flags and freeze the exact native cost-table extraction boundary;
2. verify a small hand-checkable fixture against the native estimator and unchanged writer/reader;
3. exhaustively compare final BvCompZ with the exact complete-edge DP on bounded native successor-list fixtures;
4. if a divergence is found, reproduce exact bytes/offsets/reader equality and then test GIST plus at least one other natural corpus;
5. count compression bits, selection/build time, peak memory, offsets, decoding/random-access costs, and any tuning/search cost.

Failure to find any divergence in an adequately frozen exhaustive domain, a proof of equivalence, or no residual under full cost would support STOP. Lack of an already-run result at this closing review does not.
