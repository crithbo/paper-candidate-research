## Narrow confirmed claim

On a characterized subset of natural Git histories, a budgeted Git-format-constrained constructor may create a bounded-depth delta forest and legal locality-aware layout that preserves one frozen self-contained pack/index object while extending the fully charged frontier beyond every compatible member of a preregistered current-Git 2.55 union.

This is a conditional research claim, not a finding, priority claim, universal compression claim, new-format claim, or authorization to execute.
